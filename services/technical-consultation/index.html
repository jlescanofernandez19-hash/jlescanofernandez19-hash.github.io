Fire Protection Technical Consultation

Professional fire protection guidance for design decisions, code interpretation, system requirements, and project-specific technical concerns.

We provide technical consultation for fire protection projects at various stages. From initial planning and design development to system review, troubleshooting, and project coordination with technical support for contractors, consultants, property owners, and businesses.

What We Can Assist With

Code & Standards Guidance

  • Interpretation of applicable fire protection requirements

  • NFPA-based technical guidance

  • Review of project-specific fire protection criteria

  • Identification of applicable NFPA standards

  • Comparison of design requirements against available local or national requirements

  • Assistance in identifying potential code or standards conflicts

Fire Protection System Review

  • Review of proposed fire protection system concepts

  • Review of sprinkler, fire alarm, and suppression system configurations

  • Review of fire pump and water supply requirements

  • Review of existing fire protection systems

  • Identification of potential design deficiencies or technical concerns

Design & Engineering Support

  • Preliminary design consultation

  • Design criteria development

  • System selection guidance

  • Equipment and component selection

  • Fire protection system coordination with other disciplines

  • Technical review of drawings and calculations

  • Assistance with design revisions

Troubleshooting & Technical Problem-Solving

  • Existing system performance concerns

  • Fire pump and water supply issues

  • Sprinkler system concerns

  • Fire alarm and detection issues

  • Fire suppression system concerns

  • Technical assessment of reported deficiencies

  • Recommendations for corrective actions

Inspection, Testing & Maintenance Guidance

  • Review of inspection and testing requirements

  • Fire pump testing guidance

  • Fire protection system maintenance recommendations

  • Deficiency assessment

  • Technical recommendations for system reliability and readiness

Technical Documentation

  • Technical reports

  • Design narratives

  • Scope of work

  • Technical specifications

  • Compliance documentation

  • Deficiency reports

  • BOQ/material requirements support

  • Technical recommendations

Note: Fire protection requirements vary by country, jurisdiction, building type, and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ).

Our technical consultation approach is based on internationally recognized fire protection principles and standards, including NFPA, while considering the applicable requirements and regulations of the project's location.

We can support projects internationally with technical review, fire protection guidance, design coordination, and standards-based recommendations.

What to Send Us

  • A brief description of your project or technical concern

  • Project location — country, city, and site location if available

  • Building type / occupancy — e.g., warehouse, office, hotel, residential, industrial, data center

  • Project stage — concept, design, construction, existing facility, renovation, or troubleshooting

  • Related drawings or specifications — PDF, CAD, plans, specifications, etc.

  • Existing fire protection system details, if applicable

  • Equipment details and technical data sheets, if applicable

  • Existing calculations, reports, inspection records, or test results, if available

  • Applicable codes, standards, or project specifications already provided by the client/AHJ

  • Specific question, decision, or problem you need assistance with

  • Required timeline or target date

If you're unsure what information is required, simply tell us what you're trying to achieve or what problem you're experiencing. We can help identify the information needed for the technical review.

Applicable codes and standards are identified based on the project scope, location, occupancy, system type, and applicable regulatory requirements. Our engineering and technical work may be developed with reference to internationally recognized fire protection standards, including NFPA, together with applicable local codes, regulations, project specifications, and AHJ requirements.

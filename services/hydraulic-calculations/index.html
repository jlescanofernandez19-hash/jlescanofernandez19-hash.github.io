Hydraulic Calculations

Professional hydraulic calculation services for fire protection systems to evaluate water flow, pressure requirements, pipe sizing, and overall system demand.

We provide hydraulic calculation support for water-based fire protection systems based on project requirements, design criteria, system configuration, available water supply, applicable standards, and manufacturer requirements. Our services cover hydraulic analysis, pipe sizing, system demand calculations, water supply evaluation, fire pump coordination, and technical documentation.

International Project Support

Hydraulic calculation requirements, design criteria, pipe sizing methods, equipment standards, and regulatory requirements may vary by country and project.

We can provide hydraulic calculation support based on the project's specifications, applicable standards, design criteria, manufacturer requirements, water supply information, and local project requirements.

Where required, we can work with metric or imperial units, client-specific calculation formats, specified pipe materials, approved manufacturers, and project-specific design criteria.

What to Send Us

  • Project location
  • Building type and occupancy
  • Fire protection system type
  • Fire protection drawings
  • Architectural drawings or floor plans
  • Sprinkler layout
  • Standpipe or hydrant layout, if applicable
  • Pipe sizes and material specifications, if available
  • Sprinkler head specifications
  • Hydraulic design criteria
  • Required design density and remote area, if specified
  • Water supply information
  • Flow test results, if available
  • Fire pump information and pump curve, if applicable
  • Water storage information
  • Elevation information
  • Existing hydraulic calculations, if applicable
  • Project specifications
  • Approved manufacturer or equipment requirements
  • Required calculation software or format, if specified
  • Project timeline or submission deadline

If you are unsure what information is required, send us the drawings, specifications, water supply information, previous calculations, or other documents currently available. We can review them and identify the information needed to proceed.

Important Note

Hydraulic calculations are developed based on the drawings, specifications, design criteria, water supply information, equipment data, and other project information provided at the time of calculation.

Calculation results may change following design revisions, changes in pipe sizes or system configuration, updated water supply test data, equipment substitutions, field conditions, or changes in project requirements.

Hydraulic calculation support does not constitute final design approval, product certification, authority approval, or acceptance by the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ). Final system design, equipment selection, installation, testing, and acceptance remain subject to the applicable project requirements, consultant/client approval, manufacturer requirements, local regulations, and AHJ requirements, where applicable.

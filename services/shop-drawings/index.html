Shop Drawings & As-Built Drawings

Clear, coordinated, and technically detailed fire protection drawings for construction, installation, documentation, and project closeout.

We prepare and support fire protection shop drawings and as-built documentation for new installations, system modifications, renovations, and existing facilities.

Our drawing services can support contractors, consultants, property owners, and project teams throughout the design, construction, and closeout stages of a project.

What We Can Assist With

Fire Protection Shop Drawings

  • Fire sprinkler shop drawings

  • Fire alarm and detection layouts

  • Fire suppression system layouts

  • Fire pump room layouts

  • Standpipe and hose system layouts

  • Hydrant and fire department connection layouts

  • Fire protection piping layouts

  • Riser diagrams and schematic diagrams

  • Equipment and component layouts

  • Sectional and enlarged detail drawings

  • Installation details and typical details

  • Coordination drawings for fire protection systems

As-Built Drawings

  • Preparation of as-built fire protection drawings

  • Updating approved shop drawings based on actual site installation

  • Incorporation of approved field changes and modifications

  • Documentation of installed pipe routing and equipment locations

  • Updating sprinkler head locations and system components

  • Updating fire alarm device locations and circuit information

  • Revision of riser diagrams and system schematics

  • Existing system documentation where reliable site information is available

Drawing Review & Revision

  • Review of existing shop drawings

  • Revision of drawings based on design changes

  • Incorporation of consultant or client comments

  • Coordination of fire protection drawings with architectural and MEP drawings

  • Identification of drawing inconsistencies and coordination issues

  • Conversion or updating of existing drawings

  • Preparation of revised drawing sets for construction or documentation

Typical Drawing Information

Depending on the project scope, drawings may include:

  • Floor plans and reflected ceiling plans

  • Fire protection piping layouts

  • Sprinkler head locations and coverage

  • Pipe sizes and routing

  • Valves and system components

  • Fire hose cabinets and fire department connections

  • Fire alarm devices

  • Fire suppression equipment

  • Riser diagrams

  • Isometric drawings

  • Details and sections

  • Equipment schedules

  • General notes and legends

International Project Support

Drawing requirements, drafting conventions, codes, symbols, and documentation standards may vary by country, jurisdiction, client, and project.

Our drawings can be developed based on the project requirements, available design information, applicable fire protection standards, local requirements, and client/consultant drafting standards.

Where required, we can work from client-provided templates, CAD standards, title blocks, layering conventions, symbols, and drawing requirements.

What to Send Us

To help us determine the appropriate drawing scope, please provide:

  • Project location

  • Building type and occupancy

  • Project stage

  • Existing drawings, if available

  • Architectural background plans

  • Fire protection design drawings or reference plans

  • Approved plans or specifications, if available

  • Hydraulic calculations, if applicable

  • Equipment and material information

  • Site photos or field measurements for as-built documentation

  • Markups or redline drawings showing field changes

  • Client or consultant drafting standards, if applicable

  • Required drawing format (DWG, PDF, or other)

  • Required submission or completion date

If you do not have complete drawing information, send us what you currently have. We can review the available information and identify what additional information is required to proceed.

If you're unsure what information is required, simply tell us what you're trying to achieve or what problem you're experiencing. We can help identify the information needed for the technical review.

Important Note

The accuracy of as-built documentation depends on the completeness and reliability of the information provided, including approved drawings, site measurements, field markups, photographs, and other available project records.

Applicable codes, standards, drawing requirements, and project specifications are determined based on the actual project scope and location.

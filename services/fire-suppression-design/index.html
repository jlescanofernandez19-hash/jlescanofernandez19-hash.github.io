Fire Suppression System Design

Specialized fire suppression system design for facilities requiring targeted protection of equipment, processes, and high-risk areas.

We provide technical design support for fire suppression systems based on the protected hazard, application requirements, project specifications, applicable standards, and manufacturer requirements. Our services cover system assessment, design development, calculations, equipment selection, sourcing, documentation, and coordination with related building systems.

International Project Support

Fire suppression system requirements, agent approvals, equipment standards, design methods, and regulatory requirements may vary by country and project.

We can develop technical design documentation based on the project's specifications, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, equipment approvals, and local project requirements.

Where required, we can work with metric or imperial units, client-specific drawing standards, approved manufacturer lists, specified equipment, and project-specific documentation requirements.

What to Send Us

To develop the appropriate fire suppression system design, please provide:

  • Project location
  • Building type and occupancy
  • Description of the hazard or protected area
  • Room dimensions and ceiling height
  • Architectural drawings or floor plans
  • Reflected ceiling plans, where applicable
  • Fire suppression system specifications
  • Existing fire protection drawings, if applicable
  • Existing suppression system information, if applicable
  • Equipment or manufacturer requirements
  • Approved manufacturer or vendor lists
  • Applicable project standards or specifications
  • HVAC and ventilation information, where applicable
  • Room enclosure and construction details
  • Equipment or process information for protected hazards
  • Hydraulic or agent calculation requirements, if specified
  • Required drawing format
  • Project timeline or submission deadline

If you are unsure what information is required, send us the drawings, specifications, photos, equipment information, or other documents currently available. We can review them and identify the information needed to proceed.

Important Note

Fire suppression system designs are developed based on the drawings, specifications, hazard information, equipment data, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, and other project information provided at the time of design.

Final system selection, equipment approval, installation, commissioning, and acceptance remain subject to the applicable project requirements, manufacturer requirements, consultant/client approval, local regulations, and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) requirements, where applicable.

For engineered suppression systems, final agent quantities, system configuration, and performance requirements may also require verification using the applicable manufacturer's approved design tools, calculation methods, or software.

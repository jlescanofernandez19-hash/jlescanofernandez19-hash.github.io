Fire Alarm & Detection System Design

Professional fire alarm and detection system design for the protection of buildings, occupants, equipment, and critical areas.

We provide technical design support for fire alarm and detection systems based on project requirements, building characteristics, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, and local regulatory requirements. Our services cover system assessment, device selection, system layout, design documentation, equipment sourcing, and coordination with related building systems.

International Project Support

Fire alarm requirements, device standards, equipment approvals, system configurations, and regulatory requirements may vary by country and project.

We can develop technical design documentation based on the project's specifications, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, equipment approvals, and local project requirements.

Where required, we can work with metric or imperial units, client-specific drawing standards, approved manufacturer lists, specified equipment, and project-specific documentation requirements.

What to Send Us

To develop the appropriate fire alarm and detection system design, please provide:

  • Project location
  • Building type and occupancy
  • Architectural drawings or floor plans
  • Reflected ceiling plans, where applicable
  • Electrical drawings, if available
  • Fire protection drawings, if applicable
  • Fire alarm system specifications
  • Existing fire alarm system information, if applicable
  • Existing fire alarm drawings, if applicable
  • Equipment or manufacturer requirements
  • Approved manufacturer or vendor lists
  • Applicable project standards or specifications
  • Fire suppression system information, where applicable
  • HVAC and mechanical system information, where applicable
  • Elevator information, where applicable
  • Required system interfaces
  • Required drawing format
  • Project timeline or submission deadline

If you are unsure what information is required, send us the drawings, specifications, photos, equipment information, or other documents currently available. We can review them and identify the information needed to proceed.

Important Note

Fire alarm and detection system designs are developed based on the drawings, specifications, building information, equipment data, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, and other project information provided at the time of design.

Final system selection, equipment approval, installation, programming, testing, commissioning, and acceptance remain subject to the applicable project requirements, manufacturer requirements, consultant/client approval, local regulations, and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) requirements, where applicable.

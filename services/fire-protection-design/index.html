Overall Fire Protection System Design

Comprehensive fire protection system design and coordination for buildings, facilities, and specialized applications.

We provide technical fire protection design support covering the overall planning, coordination, and integration of fire protection systems within a project. Our approach considers the building use, fire hazards, project requirements, applicable standards, system interfaces, equipment requirements, and local regulatory requirements.

International Project Support

Fire protection requirements, design practices, equipment standards, and regulatory requirements may vary by country and project.

We can provide technical design support based on the project's specifications, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, equipment approvals, and local project requirements.

Our technical work may reference internationally recognized fire protection standards, including NFPA, together with applicable local codes, regulations, project specifications, and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) requirements.

Where required, we can work with metric or imperial units, client-specific drawing standards, approved manufacturer lists, specified equipment, and project-specific documentation requirements.

What to Send Us

  • Project location

  • Building type and occupancy

  • Architectural drawings

  • Floor plans

  • Reflected ceiling plans, where applicable

  • Building sections and elevations, if available

  • Structural drawings, if applicable

  • Mechanical and HVAC drawings, if available

  • Electrical drawings, if available

  • Existing fire protection drawings, if applicable

  • Project fire protection specifications

  • Applicable project standards or design criteria

  • Fire protection system requirements

  • Fire pump and water supply information, if available

  • Hydraulic calculations, if available

  • Approved manufacturer or vendor lists

  • Existing equipment information, where applicable

  • Required drawing or documentation format

  • Project timeline or submission deadline

If you are unsure what information is required, send us the drawings, specifications, photos, existing system information, or other documents currently available. We can review them and identify the information needed to proceed.

Important Note

Fire protection system design services are developed based on the drawings, specifications, building information, hazard information, equipment data, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, and other project information provided at the time of design.

Final system selection, equipment approval, installation, testing, commissioning, and acceptance remain subject to the applicable project requirements, consultant/client approval, manufacturer requirements, local regulations, and Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) requirements, where applicable.

Where a project requires specialized engineering, professional certification, licensed design authority, or formal approval within a specific jurisdiction, the appropriate local professional or authority should be engaged as required.

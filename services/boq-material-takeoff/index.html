Fire Protection BOQ & Procurement Support

Detailed fire protection quantity take-offs, Bills of Quantities, and procurement support to help project teams estimate, source, and coordinate the materials required for their fire protection systems.

We support contractors, consultants, estimators, and project owners by translating fire protection drawings and specifications into organized material requirements for estimating, budgeting, quotation, and procurement.

Covers
  • Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
  • Material Take-Off (MTO)
  • Material & Equipment Specification Review

What We Can Assist With

Bill of Quantities (BOQ)

  • Preparation of fire protection BOQs
  • Material quantity take-offs
  • Itemized material schedules
  • Equipment and component schedules
  • Pipe, fitting, and valve quantities
  • Sprinkler head and accessory quantities
  • Fire alarm device quantities
  • Fire suppression system components
  • Fire pump and water supply equipment
  • Supports, hangers, and installation accessories
  • BOQ preparation based on client-provided templates

Material Take-Off (MTO)

  • Take-off from fire protection shop drawings
  • Take-off from design drawings
  • Take-off from PDF or CAD drawings
  • Quantification of piping, fittings, valves, equipment, and accessories
  • Area-by-area or system-by-system quantity breakdown
  • Cross-checking quantities against drawings and specifications

Material & Equipment Specification Review

  • Review of specified materials and equipment
  • Identification of required sizes, ratings, and specifications
  • Review against project specifications
  • Identification of missing or unclear material requirements
  • Organization of technical descriptions for procurement and quotation

Procurement Support

  • Preparation of material requisition lists
  • Supplier and manufacturer sourcing through our supplier network
  • Identification of suitable products based on project specifications
  • Supplier quotation requests / RFQ preparation
  • Coordination of product inquiries with suppliers
  • Collection and organization of supplier quotations
  • Comparison of quoted materials, quantities, specifications, and pricing
  • Technical comparison of proposed products
  • Review of product compliance with project specifications and applicable requirements
  • Identification of suitable alternative or equivalent products for client/consultant review
  • Lead-time and availability checking
  • Procurement planning and order coordination
  • Purchase order coordination with suppliers
  • Procurement tracking and status updates
  • Coordination of supplier documentation, technical data sheets, and certificates
  • Identification of potential material shortages, substitutions, or scope gaps
  • Coordination of material delivery to the project site, where applicable

BOQ & Drawing Cross-Check

We can cross-check available drawings, specifications, and BOQs to help identify:

  • Missing materials
  • Quantity discrepancies
  • Inconsistent material sizes
  • Unlisted equipment or components
  • Drawing-to-BOQ discrepancies
  • Potential scope gaps
  • Duplicate items
  • Changes between drawing revisions

International Project Support

Material specifications, product standards, manufacturers, units of measurement, and procurement practices may vary by country and project.

We can assist with sourcing fire protection materials and equipment through our supplier network based on the project's technical specifications, required quantities, approved manufacturers, and delivery requirements.

Where required, we can work with metric or imperial units, client-specific BOQ formats, approved vendor lists, and specified manufacturers or product standards.

What to Send Us

To prepare the appropriate BOQ or procurement support package, please provide:

  • Project location
  • Building type and occupancy
  • Fire protection drawings, if applicable
  • Architectural background drawings
  • Project specifications
  • Approved plans, if available
  • Hydraulic calculations, if applicable
  • Equipment schedules
  • Approved manufacturer or vendor lists
  • Preferred brands or product specifications
  • Existing BOQ or client template, if available
  • Supplier quotation format or RFQ requirements, if applicable
  • Required level of detail
  • Project timeline or submission deadline

If you are unsure what information is required, send us the documents currently available. We can review them and identify the information needed to proceed.

Important Note

BOQ quantities and procurement recommendations are based on the drawings, specifications, and project information provided at the time of review. Quantities may change following design revisions, approved substitutions, field conditions, or changes in project requirements.

Procurement support does not constitute final product approval or authority approval. Final material selection and procurement decisions remain subject to the project's specifications, consultant/client approval, applicable local requirements, and the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ), where applicable.

What we do

LILIN Fire Protection supports contractors, consultants, property owners, and businesses with fire protection engineering, equipment sourcing, and technical assistance.

How we work

  • Fire protection expertise applied to the actual project scope
  • Engineering support: design, calculations, drawings, and BOQ
  • Equipment sourcing through qualified manufacturers and suppliers
  • Project support from specification review to closeout
  • A commitment to reliable fire protection solutions

Applicable standards are identified according to the actual project scope. Our engineering and technical work may be developed with reference to applicable fire protection standards and project requirements.
